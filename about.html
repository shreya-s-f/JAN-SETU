The Problem We Solve

Spatial Civic Intelligence
Next-Gen 3D Digital Twin City Model
Real-time LiDAR point clouds and verified citizen spatial geocoding for zero-latency municipal response.

In rapidly expanding Indian cities like Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, and Hyderabad, millions of citizens navigate daily civic hazards: severe craters and potholes, overflowing roadside garbage dumps, broken streetlights that create dark unsafe stretches, and choked drains that flood roads in 20 minutes of rain.

Traditional grievance helplines too often feel like opaque black holes where complaints vanish without photographic proof, verified timelines, or community oversight.

JanSetu changes this paradigm completely. By turning every citizen into a civic observer and equipping municipal teams with transparent task tracking, we create mutual accountability powered by verifiable photographic proof.

Visual Proof Always

Every complaint begins with a geo-tagged defect photo, and every resolution must be completed with an authentic "After" photo.

Civic Priority Score

Our objective formula calculates affected population, hazard severity, and community upvotes to eliminate favoritism.

Community Verification

Neighbors can verify if an issue is still present or truly resolved, providing ground-truth checks on administrative claims.

Technical Architecture

JanSetu is engineered as a pure, high-performance Frontend-Only Web Application using Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript, CSS3 design tokens, and HTML5.

  • Data Store: Persistent centralized client repository in localStorage with auto-initialization and clean schema CRUD.
  • Interactive Maps: Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap with custom SVG markers and a Problem Heatmap simulator.
  • Visual Analytics: Chart.js 4.4 for department scorecards, monthly trends, and category distribution.
  • Email System: Modular emailService.js abstraction generating simulated outbound emails and UI audit logs, ready for plug-and-play backend connection.
