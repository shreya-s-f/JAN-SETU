How JanSetu Works

A transparent 4-step pipeline that transforms community complaints into verified municipal resolutions.

01

Snap & Geo-Report

Click a photo of the pothole, garbage dump, or broken streetlight. JanSetu captures verified GPS coordinates automatically.

02

Civic Priority Scoring

Our multi-factor scoring evaluates affected population, road hazard levels, and community upvotes to set urgent priorities.

03

Field Squad Dispatch

Municipal administrators assign dedicated field workers. Citizens track real-time progress notes directly on the timeline.

04

Before/After Proof

Workers upload "After" resolution photos. Citizens inspect the results, rate the quality, or reopen the complaint if incomplete.
